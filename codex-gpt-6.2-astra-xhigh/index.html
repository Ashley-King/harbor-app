Sources & map notes

Positions come from Open Waters AIS. The register includes eligible ships heard within 30 minutes of each successful check. Reports and NOAA observations refresh every 10 minutes.

True-color satellite imagery contains modified Copernicus Sentinel-2 data from 13 November 2026. Close views use USGS / USDA aerial photography. Night lighting is an artistic treatment. Ships visible in the photography are part of the basemap. Colored markers identify AIS reports.

Channel outlines come from the US Army Corps of Engineers. Terminal lists use proximity to the berth face and reported speed. Berth assignments are approximate.

Observed water level is relative to mean lower low water. Bridge clearance is the measured air gap at the Ravenel sensor. NOAA CO-OPS.

Ships use class-based 3D models with reported dimensions. Vessel classes come from AIS, Open Waters particulars, and exact IMO matches in EMSA THETIS-MRV. Where orientation or dimensions are unavailable, a position marker identifies the ship. For observation only.
